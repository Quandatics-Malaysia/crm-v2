## Purpose

P2P records the supplier-side documents required to source and pay for work
associated with a Project.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  project[Project] --> rfq[Request for quotation]
  rfq --> po[Purchase order]
  po --> pinvoice[Purchase invoice]
  pinvoice --> payment[Payment]
```

## Rules

- Supplier party is currently free text rather than a dedicated vendor master.
- Documents may link to the Project and an upstream parent document.
- The same draft, issued, settled, and cancelled states apply as O2C.
- Valid parent/child relationships are controlled by `lib/finance-kinds.ts`.
- Purchase-side documents share tenant numbering with all other finance kinds.
- Intercompany mirroring may create a purchase invoice paired with the partner's
  sales invoice.

## Extension rule

Create a Vendor module only when supplier identity needs independent lifecycle,
ownership, permissions, or master-data behavior. Until then, P2P remains a
Finance capability, not a separate plugin.
