## Information architecture

```text
Settings
├── General
│   └── Company profile and defaults
├── People
│   └── Membership behavior and organization access
├── Developers
│   └── API keys
├── Billing
│   ├── Invoicing
│   ├── Numbering
│   └── Tax
├── Taxonomy
    ├── Funnel stages
    ├── Industries and countries
    ├── Product codes
    └── Project natures
└── Platform (platform master only)
    └── Subscription, licensing, and invoices
```

Finance-specific settings are hidden when Finance is disabled. Project-nature
settings remain useful to core sales and quotations even when Projects is off.

## Responsibilities

- Company identity, logo, currency, and commercial defaults.
- Payment terms, document types, reminders, and numbering.
- Lead sources, loss reasons, industries, countries, and product codes.
- Project-nature and milestone templates.
- Pipeline stage names, probability, requirements, approval, and forecast flags.
- Intercompany partner configuration.
- API-key creation and revocation.

## Appearance defaults

Appearance is user/browser scoped rather than organization scoped. On first
visit the application starts in dark mode. The header
theme toggle stores an explicit `light` or `dark` preference in browser
`localStorage`; creating or switching organizations does not reset it.

## Membership licensing model (seat gating)

Tenant membership uses a three-state model in `membership_profiles.status`:

- `active` — currently consumes one customer seat.
- `invited` — exists in the tenant but is outside current seat gate.
- `disabled` — intentionally deactivated while keeping history.

Seat decisions are evaluated from:

- `tenant_settings.subscription_status` (`active`, `trial`, `paused`, `expired`, `cancelled`)
- `tenant_settings.subscription_starts_at`
- `tenant_settings.subscription_ends_at`
- `tenant_settings.subscription_seat_limit` (`NULL` means unlimited)

When a tenant is over seat limit or outside the active subscription window:

- New adds from **Team → Add member** become `invited` (not active access).
- New sign-ups from pending invites or domain auto-join become `invited`.
- Reactivation (`active`) is allowed only when the gate is available.
- Existing customer sessions and API keys cannot use tenant data after the
  licence expires. The platform master keeps access so the tenant can be renewed.

This lets you enforce strict seat billing while still allowing accounts to queue under
an invite.

The seeded **Developer** role is intentionally not a licensing bypass. Developers
can test business modules and read internal documentation, but cannot manage
tenant users, roles, settings, or subscriptions. When active they consume a seat;
setting their membership to `disabled` releases that seat while preserving history.
The platform-master account never consumes a customer seat.

Only the platform master can open **Settings → Subscription**. The operational
flow is deliberately short:

1. Choose the plan, total customer seats, and any validity period. The form
   defaults to a one-year contract, with shortcuts for 30 days and six months.
2. Enter the monthly price per seat. The contract value is calculated as
   `seats × monthly rate × month-equivalent`, plus optional tax. Complete
   monthly cycles have a factor of `1`; a partial final cycle is prorated by
   active days divided by the days in that anchored monthly cycle.
3. Choose monthly collection milestones (the default) or one upfront
   collection. Set the first collection date and review the generated schedule.
4. Select **Issue invoice and seats**. The issued invoice and its collection
   milestones are recorded, and the
   selected access is granted immediately; payment is handled outside the CRM.

Each issue replaces the tenant's current seat total and validity window. A one-year
contract can therefore have twelve monthly collection milestones without creating
twelve separate licences. Milestone amounts always reconcile to the invoice total;
the final milestone reflects any partial-month proration and absorbs cent rounding.
There is no draft, payment-reference,
or mark-paid step. The issued seat count must cover all active customer users.

The platform master also creates customer organizations from the organization
switcher. Creation sets the initial plan, seat count, validity dates, and optional
initial users. Each initial user is assigned one of the seeded roles (Owner, Admin,
Developer, Manager, Senior Rep, Rep, or Viewer). Tenant users cannot create new
organizations.

## Extension rule

Place a setting under the business area it configures. Do not create a new
top-level settings section for one field. Plugin-specific settings must
disappear with the plugin while their stored values remain intact.
